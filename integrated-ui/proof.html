      How this proof is checked

      The fingerprint is a SHA-256 of the work log, the price list and the approved lines. It changes if any of them changes. It does not prove who sent the invoice. The check above reads the invoice from PayPal each time this page loads and compares its items, quantities, rates, currency and total with the approved lines.
